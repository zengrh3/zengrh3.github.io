Ruihong Zeng (曾锐鸿)

MBZUAI, Abu Dhabi, UAE

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI, advised by Prof. Preslav Nakov and Prof. Xiuying Chen. I completed my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Shangsong Liang.

My research focuses on memory and continual adaptation in LLM agents. I study how agents turn experience into reusable knowledge, coordinate through shared memory, and adapt across long-horizon tasks. I also work on foundation-model evaluation, multimodal fact-checking, and machine unlearning.

Research experience

  • Research Intern · Institute of Foundation Models

    Jul 2026–present

    Supervised by Haonan Li. I refine task specifications and scoring rubrics and develop a capability taxonomy to characterize evaluation coverage. I also investigate context compaction methods that preserve task state and prior decisions across long-horizon workflows.

  • Research Intern · UKP Lab, TU Darmstadt

    Mar–Jun 2026

    Supervised by Prof. Iryna Gurevych. I worked on MIC, a multimodal fact-checking framework for detecting and explaining image–claim inconsistencies using visual evidence and world knowledge.
